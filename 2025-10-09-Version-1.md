I began this project as part of an Independent Study assignment for my English class: pick a project of your choice, work on it for the next few months, and present it at the end of April. I wanted to create something engineering-related that could help people in real life, which led me to this project.

## What I was trying to accomplish

My goal is to build an affordable arm using inexpensive electronics, 3D-printed components, and easily obtainable materials.

## Progress

I began by researching how current prosthetics work, how ancient structures like the Pantheon held up for so long (for finger design), how much current prosthetics cost, and what types of materials I could use. Based on my research, I made a sketch of a prototype:
