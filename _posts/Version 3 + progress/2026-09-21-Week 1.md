I began by creating a new CAD design. It has looser joints, a new thumb piece that uses a large cylinder to attach to the hand, moving parts for the ring finger and pinky (inspired by [this 3D-printed robotic hand](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vgtJY_rJWzo)), and holes to pass the elastic strings through, so I don't have to glue them to the hand anymore.
