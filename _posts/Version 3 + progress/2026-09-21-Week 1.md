I first began by creating a new design for the CAD. This new design would have looser joints, a new design for the thumb piece that would use a large cylinder to attach it to the hand, have moving parts for the ring finger and pinky(inspired by this design: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vgtJY_rJWzo&pp=ygUXM2QgcHJpbnRlZCByb2JvdGljIGhhbmQ%3D), and have holes I can pass the elastic strings through, so that I don't have to glue them to the hand anymore.
