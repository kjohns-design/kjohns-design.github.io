When I was working on the first version, I didn't take the project too seriously. I just wanted a hand that moved fine and did nothing else. After getting obsessed with it, I decided to take it to the next level during the summer.

## Progress

I first redesigned the hand: I added a thumb piece, made the hand much smaller, printed the three-layer design all at once rather than as separate parts, added holes for the fishing line to pass through the hand and fingers, and used elastic string instead of rubber bands. This is the new design:

## The electronics

I wanted my hand to be different from ones made by others, so I used pressure sensors instead of EMG sensors to control the hand movements, and I created an artificial pain system. It uses an MPU6050 accelerometer to detect too-quick movement and strain gauges to detect high strain on the pipes.

<div class="shots">
  <img src="{{ img }}IMG5258%20(1).png" alt="Pressure sensors, accelerometer, and strain gauges" loading="lazy" />
</div>

I assembled all of the electronics on a breadboard after soldering the headers on certain parts.
